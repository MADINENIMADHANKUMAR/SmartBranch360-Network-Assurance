=======================================================
FAULT CARD 02 - Missing VLAN on Trunk
=======================================================
VLAN affected     : VLAN 20 (Guest)
Injected by       : Removing VLAN 20 from the allowed
                    VLAN list on the SW1 trunk port
                    (switchport trunk allowed vlan
                    remove 20)

SYMPTOM
  Guest PCs and the wireless AP lose all connectivity.
  They cannot get a DHCP address and cannot reach
  anything, even though VLAN 20 still exists on the
  switch.

ROOT CAUSE
  Even though VLAN 20 is configured on the switch, its
  traffic is no longer allowed to cross the trunk link
  to the router, so it is isolated on that switch only.

HOW THE PYTHON TOOL FINDS IT
  Option 3 (VLAN & IP Audit) reads "show interfaces
  trunk", collects every VLAN ID in the "Vlans allowed
  on trunk" table, and checks each required VLAN from
  the plan against that list:
      [FAIL] VLAN 20 (Guest) missing on trunk

FIX
  On the trunk port (e.g. SW1 Fa0/1):
      switchport trunk allowed vlan add 20

VERIFY
  show interfaces trunk
